DESIGN-AI-DAILY

An English and Korean design journal supported by a private editorial automation tool. It connects source-checked design work with AI research, clearly separating observation, interpretation and unexecuted ideas.

What the application does

The existing Python application collects approved public sources, prepares English and Korean drafts, runs automated editorial checks, and keeps a durable private record to prevent duplicate drafts. Its Blogger integration has already stored a limited pair of private test drafts. Broader generation and unattended operation are not yet enabled.

Google authorization

Google authorization is for the operator's own Blogger account, not a reader sign-in service. The Blogger permission can manage blogs across the authorized account; it is not a Google-enforced draft-only or single-blog permission. The application's own allowlist restricts writes to the designated DESIGN-AI-DAILY blog, and its implemented publishing path permits drafts only. Existing posts are not automatically published, edited or deleted.

Readers do not need to authorize Google access to read public information pages. Google passwords are entered only on Google's official authentication screens.

Editorial status and availability

Automated QA is not human approval. Articles require human editorial review and a separate publication decision. Production OAuth configuration, scheduled operation and wider publication remain pending; this page does not claim those steps are complete.
